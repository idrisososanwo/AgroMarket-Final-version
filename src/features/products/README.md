# Products Domain

## Responsibilities
- Agricultural produce taxonomy, categories, and standard packaging units (bags, baskets, crates, kg, tonnes)
- Product verification and specifications
- Shelf life, harvest date, and quality gradings

## Critical Product Rules
- **Prohibited Items Policy**: Pig/pork products (pork, bacon, ham, lard, swine) must NEVER be included in product categories, marketplace examples, Shared Purchase examples, recommendations, or seed data.
