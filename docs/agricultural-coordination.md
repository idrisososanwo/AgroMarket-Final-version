# AgroMarket Phase 3.10: Multi-Party Agricultural Coordination & Supply Commitment Foundation

## 1. Purpose & Product Boundary

AgroMarket functions as an asset-light agricultural coordination and commerce infrastructure layer. In fragmented agricultural supply chains, large commercial demands (e.g. food processors, supermarket chains, industrial aggregators, institutional catering, and hotel groups) frequently exceed the productive capacity of any single smallholder farmer or rancher.

Phase 3.10 establishes the deterministic foundation for **multi-party agricultural coordination** without autonomous procurement or capital exposure:
- Represents structured coordination opportunities where demand is aggregated from or fulfilled by multiple independent producers.
- Manages discrete supply commitments without converting commitments into premature financial orders or auto-dispatched liabilities.
- Operates under strict server-authoritative governance, privacy boundaries, and halal anti-pork invariants.

```
DEMAND (B2B Demand or Aggregation Target)
  │
  ▼
COORDINATION OPPORTUNITY (Requirements & Delivery Window)
  │
  ├── Producer A Offer (1,500 kg) ──► Governance Gate ──► Accepted
  ├── Producer B Offer (1,000 kg) ──► Governance Gate ──► Accepted
  └── Producer C Offer (2,500 kg) ──► Governance Gate ──► Accepted
  │
  ▼
COVERAGE EVALUATION (Deterministic Canonical Quantity Accounting)
  │
  ▼
CONFIRMATION & ADVISORY READINESS (Processing / Logistics / Inspection)
  │
  ▼
FULFILMENT VERIFICATION (Physical Intake Receipt Recorded)
```

## 2. Core Domain Model & Lifecycles

### 2.1 Coordination Opportunity Lifecycle
Managed via `agricultural_coordination_opportunities`:
- `DRAFT`: Initial specification formulation.
- `OPEN`: Published and accepting producer supply commitment offers.
- `COORDINATING`: Active multi-party offers under review.
- `PARTIALLY_COMMITTED`: Accepted commitments > 0 kg, but < 100% of required quantity.
- `FULLY_COMMITTED`: 100% of required quantity covered by accepted supply commitments.
- `IN_FULFILMENT`: Delivery window active; physical fulfillments under receipt.
- `COMPLETED`: 100% of required quantity physically fulfilled.
- `CANCELLED`: Terminal state initiated by coordinator with required justification.
- `EXPIRED`: Delivery window expired before complete commitment or fulfillment.
- `CONSTRAINED`: Logistics or biosecurity advisory flags temporary movement limitation.

### 2.2 Supply Commitment Lifecycle
Managed via `agricultural_supply_commitments`:
- `PROPOSED`: Draft offer generated by producer.
- `OFFERED`: Submitted by producer for coordinator review.
- `ACCEPTED`: Reviewed and accepted into coordination by coordinator (atomically checked against capacity).
- `CONFIRMED`: Producer confirms harvest readiness for delivery.
- `FULFILMENT_PENDING`: Dispatched or scheduled for intake inspection.
- `FULFILLED`: Physical intake verified by coordinator.
- **Terminal States**: `WITHDRAWN`, `REJECTED`, `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`, `FAILED`.

## 3. Quantity Accounting & Concurrency Safeguards

### 3.1 Mathematical Quantity Normalization
- All commodity quantities are converted to canonical mass (KG) using `normalizeSupplyToDemandUnit` from `@/features/supply-matching/units`.
- Discrete packaging (e.g., 50kg bags, crates) is strictly validated against physical density rules; incompatible conversions fail closed (`INCOMPATIBLE_UNIT`).

### 3.2 Concurrency & Over-Commitment Prevention
When multiple producers attempt to commit supply simultaneously, race conditions could cause total accepted commitments to exceed the required quantity:
$$\sum \text{accepted\_commitments} + \text{new\_commitment} \le \text{required\_quantity}$$
To guarantee safety:
1. **Database Row Lock**: PostgreSQL function `atomic_accept_supply_commitment` locks both the commitment row and parent coordination opportunity row using `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`.
2. **Deterministic Rejection**: If acceptance would exceed required capacity, the database rolls back the transaction and returns `OVERCOMMITMENT_BLOCKED`.
3. **In-Memory Test Parity**: The application data layer implements an identical atomic capacity check (`validateCommitmentCapacity`) during unit testing.

## 4. Privacy & Information Isolation

Producers contributing to a shared coordination opportunity are competitors or independent operators. The platform enforces strict data isolation:
- **Masked Peer Identities**: Peer producers only see aggregate commitments and anonymized labels (e.g. `"Producer (Oyo)"`).
- **Sanitized Commercial Terms**: Private producer notes, internal pricing discussions, and rejection reasons are stripped from peer views (`sanitizeSupplyCommitmentForViewer`).
- **No Direct Contact Exposure**: Phone numbers and exact sub-meter GPS coordinates are strictly forbidden in public queries and deep links.

## 5. System Boundaries & Governance Integration

### 5.1 Intelligence Integration (Advisory Only)
- Supply matching (`Phase 2.6`) and Procurement intelligence (`Phase 2.7`) feed candidate opportunities into coordination.
- **Strict Distinction**:
  $$\text{MATCH} \ne \text{COMMITMENT}$$
  $$\text{RECOMMENDATION} \ne \text{COMMITMENT}$$
  The system never autonomously commits supply based on an intelligence match.

### 5.2 Phase 3.8 Governance Enforcement
- All consequential mutations (`createCoordinationOpportunityAction`, `offerSupplyCommitmentAction`, `acceptSupplyCommitmentAction`) pass through `evaluateGovernancePolicy`.
- Any policy decision of `DENY` or unfulfilled `REQUIRE_HUMAN_APPROVAL` halts execution immediately.

### 5.3 Payment & Financial Boundary
- **Zero Automated Transactions**: A commitment is NOT an invoice, purchase order, or escrow deposit.
- AgroMarket does not move money, offer trade credit, insurance, or autonomous payouts during coordination.

### 5.4 Anti-Pork Halal Invariant
- Every commodity and title input is verified against the denylist regex at the database constraint level (`chk_no_pork_*`), Zod validation level, and server action level (`assertNoProhibitedProduceCoordination`).

### 5.5 Disease & Security Boundaries
- Outbreak and corridor disruptions provide advisory warning badges on the opportunity detail page.
- AgroMarket does not provide tactical security movement routes or order animal culling.

---

## 6. Known Limitations & Explicitly Deferred Features

1. **Automated Multi-Leg Freight Optimization**: Deferred to dedicated logistics operational phases. Pickups are recorded manually.
2. **Automated Split-Payment Settlement**: Commitments do not execute financial transactions; payment is handled via established checkout/settlement flows once fulfilled.
3. **Escrow Custody**: AgroMarket does not take custodial possession of funds or hold agricultural escrow.
